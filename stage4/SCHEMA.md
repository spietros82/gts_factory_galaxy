Identity scope: bottle_id and station_id are local names; their globally unique identity is `(line_id, session_id, bottle_id, station_id)`. Frame and batch UUIDs are globally unique; queued IDs are reserved at admission, not merely after files appear. Results are never indexed by bare bottle_id. Reset/re-add creates a new session even if a local bottle number repeats.

Native heartbeat retains at most96recent neutral `{batch_id, acquisition_wait_ms}` samples plus oldest_acquisition_seconds. Backend joins only matching recorded frames to report trigger_to_result_p50/p95/p99_ms and sample count; absent matches remain N/A in UI. `end_to_end_*` remains the original capture→result definition for compatibility. Final trace wait is milliseconds; the frozen9ea trace correction is explicitly version-specific. Live metric histories span bounded lifetime samples, while report figures use declared steady windows.

Per-line summary includes inspection_health and rate_elapsed_seconds. Rate denominator is current registry/session registration, independent of backend lifetime. Health derives only from current matching batch results, freshness, pause and backend availability; it does not change inspection decisions or infer mechanical cause. GUI clears retired review selection onresize/reset and clears stale service summaries onoutage.
