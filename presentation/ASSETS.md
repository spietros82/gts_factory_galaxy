# Stage 1 asset provenance

No external assets, downloads, image-generation service or new renderer packages used.

- Machinery and tank shapes: original Factory Galaxy procedural geometry, reused and augmented locally. Existing project ownership; fictional Braemac demo identity.
- Brushed steel, epoxy floor, conveyor and carton textures: small seeded mathematical RGB textures generated by BreweryPresentation.cs. No photographs or manufacturer logos.
- Lager / IPA / Stout labels: locally authored five-by-seven pixel glyphs and geometric colour bands, generated into reusable 256×128 textures. All brewery/product branding is fictional.
- Glass bottle mesh: locally authored lathed mesh with UV coordinates. Contents are a simple cylinder and do not simulate optics/fluid dynamics.
- Runtime signs: Unity TextMesh and existing built-in font facilities. Unity engine/shaders/font distribution remains governed by the installed Unity licence; no font files or Unity binaries are committed to GitHub.
- Rendering: existing Built-in pipeline and Standard shader, Unity6000.6.5f1. No paid assets, third-party textures, model weights or pretrained assets introduced.

These records describe provenance; they do not grant a general licence to the wider Braemac project or third-party Unity dependencies. No new open-source licence has been asserted on Pietro's behalf.
